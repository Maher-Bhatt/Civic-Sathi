# Civic Sathi: Complete Platform Feature & Architecture Matrix

This document provides a hyper-detailed, line-by-line analysis of every feature, module, and interconnected system within the Civic Sathi platform. The platform is distributed across a Python FastAPI backend and four distinct React/TypeScript (TanStack) frontends. 

---

## 1. Public Portal (`apps/public`)
**Target Audience**: Everyday Citizens, Community Organizers
**Purpose**: Civic engagement, issue reporting, transparency, and participatory governance.

### Core Routes & Features
*   **`/` (Home & Landing)**
    *   **Feature**: Marketing landing page and "How it works" guide.
    *   **Connection**: Public-facing, static content with calls-to-action routing to `/report` or `/login`.
*   **`/hub` (Community Hub)**
    *   **Feature 1 - Environmental Metrics**: Displays real-time API-driven data for Air Quality (AQI), Weather/Temperature, and Water Supply status.
    *   **Feature 2 - Civic Gamification**: "Top Citizen Contributors" leaderboard. Rewards active citizens for reporting valid issues.
    *   **Feature 3 - Participatory Budgeting Preview**: Shows top community projects needing citizen votes.
*   **`/projects` (Participatory Budgeting System)**
    *   **Feature**: Citizens browse proposed municipal projects (e.g., Solar Streetlights, Park Revitalization).
    *   **Function**: Category filtering (Infrastructure, Health, Sanitation). Citizens cast votes to influence municipal spending. Visual progress bars track funding/voting goals.
    *   **Connection**: Informs the Municipality's `/tenders` system on what projects to prioritize based on democratic demand.
*   **`/map` (Civic Map)**
    *   **Feature**: Interactive geospatial map mapping out active civic issues in the user's vicinity.
    *   **Function**: Uses Turf.js and Leaflet to render heatmaps and clustering.
*   **`/report` (Issue Reporting)**
    *   **Feature**: Primary gateway for citizens to file complaints.
    *   **Function**: Supports manual form entry and AI-assisted drafting. Integrates device geolocation and camera/image uploads for proof.
    *   **Connection**: POSTs to backend `complaints.py`. Automatically triggers `ai.py` (NLP triage) in the backend to categorize the issue and route it to the correct department.
*   **`/complaints` & `/complaint/$id` (My Complaints & Tracking)**
    *   **Feature**: Personal dashboard of submitted issues.
    *   **Function**: Shows the immutable timeline of an issue (Reported → Triaged → Assigned to Contractor → Resolved).
*   **`/profile` (Citizen Identity)**
    *   **Feature**: Manages user details, addresses, and notification preferences.
*   **`/consent` (Sathi Sahamati - DEPA)**
    *   **Feature**: India DEPA (Data Empowerment and Protection Architecture) consent management.
    *   **Function**: Allows citizens to cryptographically grant or revoke permission for cross-department data sharing.
*   **`/contractors` (Public Transparency)**
    *   **Feature**: Public ledger of contractor ratings and SLA performance. Holds contractors accountable by exposing their reliability scores.

## 2. Municipality Portal (`apps/municipality`)
**Target Audience**: Ward Officers, Department Heads, Municipal Staff
**Purpose**: Triage, assignments, orchestration, and local administration.

### Core Routes & Features
*   **`/_auth/dashboard` (Live Command Center)**
    *   **Feature**: Real-time KPI metrics. Tracks open complaints, average resolution times, and SLA breach warnings.
*   **`/_auth/analytics` & `/_auth/civic-charts`**
    *   **Feature**: Deep-dive data visualizations using D3/Recharts.
    *   **Function**: Predictive hotspot analysis, seasonal civic issue trends (e.g., predicting waterlogging before monsoons).
*   **`/_auth/smart-composer` (AI Multi-Department Case Composer)**
    *   **Feature**: Agentic AI tool for officials.
    *   **Function**: Takes a complex civic emergency (e.g., "Water Main Burst & Road Cave-in") and automatically sequences it into multiple sovereign tickets for different departments (Water Board, Roadways, Traffic Police).
    *   **Connection**: Calls backend `ai.py` and `integrations.py` to dispatch webhooks via the Sathi Setu interoperability framework.
*   **`/_auth/live-orchestration` & `/_auth/case/$id` (Digital Case Passport)**
    *   **Feature**: Macro-interoperability viewer.
    *   **Function**: Tracks the real-time status of cross-departmental tickets generated by the Smart Composer. Shows dependencies (e.g., Roadway repair is *blocked by* Water pipe repair).
*   **`/_auth/ai-triage` (Smart Queue)**
    *   **Feature**: View of incoming public complaints that have been auto-categorized and severity-scored by the backend ML models. Officials approve or override the AI's suggestions.
*   **`/_auth/complaints`**
    *   **Feature**: Standard ticketing system to manually assign complaints to specific contractors.
*   **`/_auth/tenders` & `/_auth/work-orders` (Procurement System)**
    *   **Feature**: Financial & Operational control.
    *   **Function**: Municipalities publish tenders (based on Citizen Participatory Budgeting). Review bids from Contractors. Convert winning bids into Work Orders.
    *   **Connection**: Syncs directly with Contractor Portal's tender and work-order dashboards.
*   **`/_auth/administration` & `/_auth/settings`**
    *   **Feature**: Manage ward configurations, internal staff accounts, and departmental routing rules.

## 3. Admin Portal (`apps/admin`)
**Target Audience**: Super Admins, State-Level Directors, System Engineers
**Purpose**: Global oversight, platform integrity, AI tuning, and master data management.

### Core Routes & Features
*   **`/admin/dashboard` & `/admin/state-command-center`**
    *   **Feature**: God-view of the entire platform across multiple cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, etc.). Aggregated statistics.
*   **`/admin/integration-hub`**
    *   **Feature**: Monitors API gateways and Webhook health for external government connections (e.g., Police CAD systems, Fire Dept systems).
*   **`/admin/mdm` (Master Data Management)**
    *   **Feature**: Configure foundational geographical data (Zones, Wards, City polygons).
    *   **Connection**: Feeds the location-based routing system for the entire platform.
*   **`/admin/ai-oversight`**
    *   **Feature**: MLOps dashboard.
    *   **Function**: Monitors the accuracy of the NLP triage model. Admins can view "AI Confidence Scores" and retrain models if accuracy drops below thresholds.
*   **`/admin/trust-safety`**
    *   **Feature**: Platform moderation.
    *   **Function**: Tools to ban abusive users, filter spam complaints, and ensure platform integrity.
*   **`/admin/exceptions` & `/admin/audit-logs`**
    *   **Feature**: Compliance and escalation.
    *   **Function**: Immutable audit trails of every database action. Highlights severe SLA breaches that require state-level intervention.
*   **`/admin/smart-composer` & `/admin/case/$id`**
    *   **Feature**: Identical to Municipality tools but granted global cross-city permissions for testing and state-level orchestration.

## 4. Contractor Portal (`apps/contractor`)
**Target Audience**: Private Contractors, Field Workers, Vetted Agencies
**Purpose**: Job execution, bidding, reputation tracking, and proof-of-work.

### Core Routes & Features
*   **`/contractor/dashboard`**
    *   **Feature**: Operational overview showing active work orders, pending deadlines, and incoming tender alerts.
*   **`/contractor/tenders`**
    *   **Feature**: Bidding marketplace.
    *   **Function**: Contractors review open RFPs (Requests for Proposal) published by the Municipality and submit competitive financial bids.
    *   **Connection**: Bids are sent to the Municipality's `/tenders` evaluation system.
*   **`/contractor/work-orders`**
    *   **Feature**: Job execution tracking.
    *   **Function**: Contractors accept assigned issues. Once fixed, they upload "Proof of Work" (images/notes).
    *   **Connection**: Updates the public citizen's `/complaint/$id` timeline to "Resolved".
*   **`/contractor/performance` (Reputation Engine)**
    *   **Feature**: Dynamic scoring system.
    *   **Function**: Calculates the contractor's SLA compliance rate, deduction penalties for late work, and aggregates public citizen feedback into a definitive "Trust Score".
    *   **Connection**: This score is exposed to the Municipality when evaluating future bids, and to the Public on the `/contractors` transparency page.

## 5. Backend Architecture (`backend/app/api/v1/routes`)
**Purpose**: The central nervous system connecting all 4 portals, handling business logic, machine learning, and database transactions.

### Core Services & Routers
*   **`auth.py` (Security Layer)**
    *   Issues JWTs and enforces strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). Ensures a Citizen cannot access Municipality APIs, and a Municipality cannot access Super Admin APIs.
*   **`ai.py` & `triage.py` (Machine Learning Engine)**
    *   Houses the NLP classification models. Automatically detects the "Category" (e.g., Water, Road) and "Severity" (High/Medium/Low) of incoming text/audio complaints. Identifies potential duplicate complaints.
*   **`complaints.py` & `cases.py` (State Machine)**
    *   Manages the complex lifecycle of civic issues. Enforces rules (e.g., a complaint cannot be marked 'Resolved' without an attached Work Order).
*   **`procurement.py` (Financial Engine)**
    *   Handles the logic for Tenders, Bids, and Contractor assignments. Simulates transparent smart-contract style procurement.
*   **`reputation.py` (Algorithm)**
    *   Runs the background math that penalizes contractors for missed SLAs and rewards them for early completions.
*   **`integrations.py` & `mock_departments.py` (Macro-Interoperability)**
    *   Simulates external sovereign APIs (e.g., Maharashtra State Police API). Listens for webhook dispatches from the "Smart Composer" and responds with external ticket IDs.
*   **`analytics.py` (Data Pipeline)**
    *   Aggregates millions of rows of mock civic data to serve rapid KPI metrics to the Admin and Municipality dashboards.
*   **`mdm.py` & `cities.py` (Geography)**
    *   Serves GeoJSON polygons and ward boundaries for the interactive maps in all frontends.
