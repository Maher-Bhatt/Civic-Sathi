# SIH Demo Video Script: Civic-Sathi (2m 30s)

**Goal:** In 2 minutes and 30 seconds, you need to prove your system is beautiful, functional, and tackles the core problem of civic corruption and inefficiency through AI and transparency.

**Tone:** High-energy, professional, fast-paced.

---

### **0:00 - 0:15 | The Hook (15 seconds)**
*Visual: Start on the Public Portal Landing Page, showing the beautiful glassmorphism heritage UI.*
**Speaker:** "Welcome to Civic-Sathi. Indian cities face a massive trust deficit in civic infrastructure. Citizens report issues, but have no visibility into how tax money is spent to fix them. We’ve built a completely integrated, AI-powered ecosystem connecting Citizens, Municipalities, and Contractors into one transparent pipeline."

### **0:15 - 0:45 | Public Portal & AI Triage (30 seconds)**
*Visual: Switch to a Citizen filing a complaint, then jump to the Municipality Triage Dashboard showing AI grouping.*
**Speaker:** "It starts with the citizen. A user reports a dangerous pothole. Instead of creating isolated tickets, our Backend AI engine instantly groups duplicate complaints into high-impact 'Issue Clusters.' The Municipality sees these clusters prioritized by severity and risk, instantly turning citizen frustration into actionable procurement data."

### **0:45 - 1:15 | Procurement & Tender Bidding (30 seconds)**
*Visual: Contractor Portal → Tender Marketplace. Show a contractor placing a bid.*
**Speaker:** "Once a cluster is approved, a Tender is auto-generated. Contractors log into their dedicated portal to bid. Here, transparency is king. Contractors upload technical proposals and GST documents. The Municipality officers evaluate these bids on technical scores and past performance metrics generated by our system, awarding the contract fairly."

### **1:15 - 1:55 | GPS Evidence & Officer Approval (40 seconds)**
*Visual: Contractor Work Order Screen → Clicking 'Check-in at Site' (GPS) → Uploading Evidence. Then switch to Municipality Portal showing 'Pending Approval'.*
**Speaker:** "This is where we eliminate corruption. To get paid, contractors must submit field evidence tied to specific BOQ milestones. Our system enforces **Geo-fenced check-ins**—they cannot upload photos unless their GPS matches the complaint coordinates. 
Once submitted, the Municipal Officer reviews the GPS-stamped evidence. They can approve the milestone, or reject it for rework. Only verified milestones unlock the billing phase."

### **1:55 - 2:15 | Trust, Safety & SLA Penalties (20 seconds)**
*Visual: Admin Portal → Contractor Ecosystem Dashboard & SLA Penalties.*
**Speaker:** "For Super Admins, Civic-Sathi tracks everything. If a contractor misses their target completion date, the system auto-calculates Liquidated Damages and SLA penalties. We maintain a Tri-Party scorecard for every contractor, averaging citizen ratings, officer ratings, and AI analysis to automatically blacklist bad actors."

### **2:15 - 2:30 | The Citizen Loop (15 seconds)**
*Visual: Public Portal → Complaint Tracker showing the "Procurement Journey".*
**Speaker:** "Finally, we close the loop. The citizen gets a notification that their issue was fixed. They can view the complete procurement journey—which contractor won the bid, how much was spent, and before-and-after photos. This is Civic-Sathi: Smart Governance, Absolute Transparency."

---

## 🎬 **Pro-Tips for Recording**

1. **Pre-open all 4 tabs:** Have the Public, Municipality, Admin, and Contractor portals open in 4 separate browser windows so you can Alt-Tab instantly. No waiting for logins.
2. **Pre-fill Data:** Have a work order already in `IN_PROGRESS` state on the contractor portal so you can just click "Upload Evidence" immediately.
3. **Pacing:** 2m30s is very short. Do not explain *how* the code works (React/FastAPI). Only explain *what the user is seeing* and *why it solves corruption*.
4. **Practice the GPS click:** The geolocation popup might take a second. When you click "Check-in at site", keep talking while the browser grabs the coordinates.
